Open since the corner needed a good table

Cooking that doesn't hurry.

Paulo started as a single wood-fired oven and a short list of dishes worth doing properly. That's still the idea. Pasta is rolled by hand most mornings, the oven runs on real hardwood, and the wine list is short on purpose — every bottle earned its place by the glass, poured by someone who can tell you why.

We're not trying to be everything. We're trying to be the table you come back to.
